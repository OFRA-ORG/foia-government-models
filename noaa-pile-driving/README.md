# NOAA Fisheries (NMFS) — Multi-Species Pile Driving Calculator

**Agency:** U.S. Department of Commerce, National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration — NOAA Fisheries (National Marine Fisheries Service), Office of Protected Resources
**FOIA Request Number:** DOC-NOAA-2026-002009
**Date of Final Response:** August 2026 (received 8/12/2026; granted in full, one record enclosed)

## Description

The Multi-Species Pile Driving Calculator is a spreadsheet tool developed by the NOAA Fisheries Office of Protected Resources to estimate the underwater-sound effect distances (isopleths) at which pile-driving noise may exceed injury and behavioral thresholds for ESA-listed species and other protected marine taxa. It is implemented in Microsoft Excel, so the acoustic-propagation logic lives in the workbook formulas.

## Contents

- `2026-002009 - UNLOCKED BLANK Multi-Species (AUG 2025) ST public (508).xlsx` — the calculator, released unlocked in native Excel form. It contains the live impact and vibratory calculator/report sheets (200+ formulas across the acoustic-model logic), plus embedded instructions, an acronym sheet, and proxy sound-level reference tables. This is the "blank" distribution: it ships without project inputs, and entering pile-driving parameters drives the calculation.
