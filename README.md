# FOIA Government Models

This repository contains source code and artifacts from U.S. federal government models obtained through Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests. All materials are U.S. Government works and are in the public domain under 17 U.S.C. § 105.

|-----------|--------|-------|

| [`epa-scghg`](epa-scghg/) | U.S. Environmental Protection Agency | Social Cost of Greenhouse Gases (SC-GHG) |
| [`ers-season-average-price`](ers-season-average-price/) | USDA Economic Research Service | Season-Average Price / Market Year Average Price Forecast Model |

## About

Each model directory contains:
- The government-released artifacts exactly as received
- A `README.md` with provenance details (agency, FOIA request number, date received)

## Know of a model that should be here?

If you are aware of a non-public federal government model that should be made openly available — whether through a FOIA request, a public records effort, or another avenue — I'd like to hear from you. Please reach out to me directly at cgoodrich@americafirstpolicy.com.
